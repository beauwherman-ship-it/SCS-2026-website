Guests enjoy tastings from every competing community, live voting, a silent auction featuring "Gifts from the Chef" dining experiences, sponsor activations, and a community table decorating competition — all closing with chef storytelling and awards.

The Experience

Taste, vote, and make a difference

Senior Chef Showdown brings together Central Florida's senior living communities for an evening of culinary talent, friendly competition, and community spirit — all in support of Seniors First.

Guests sample every competing dish, cast a vote for their favorite, and help fund programs that empower older adults to live with dignity and independence.

The Program

Stories behind the dishes

As tasting and voting wind down, our emcee brings the room together for chef storytelling and the judges' scoring — connecting every dish back to the mission it supports.

The evening closes with the judging panel's results, a live guest vote, and the presentation of the Senior Cup to this year's champion.

Our Beneficiary

Celebrating 60 Years of Seniors First

60 Years of Changing Lives

Serving Central Florida's seniors since 1966

Founded in 1966, Seniors First is the largest social services organization in metro Orlando, dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for seniors through Meals on Wheels, in-home care, medical equipment loans, and community programs that help seniors live independently and with dignity. This year marks Seniors First’s 60th anniversary — and Senior Chef Showdown 2026 is proud to celebrate with a “Sweet 60” goal of raising $60,000, a dollar for every year of service. Net proceeds from the event go directly to Seniors First.
