Our Mission

Culinary competition with a purpose

Senior Chef Showdown, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization founded to raise funds and awareness for services that support Central Florida's senior community. Each year, culinary teams from local senior living communities compete in a live tasting competition in front of guests, judges, and sponsors — all in support of a chosen local beneficiary.

In 2026, we're celebrating Seniors First's 60th anniversary with a Birthday-themed event built around one shared ingredient: honey. Every competing team incorporates honey into an original dish, and every dollar raised works toward our $60,000 Sweet 60 goal.

How It Works
01

Compete

Senior living communities enter a culinary team, incorporate the year's common ingredient into an original dish, and compete for the Senior Cup.

02

Taste & Vote

Guests sample every dish and cast a live vote for their favorite, alongside a panel of judges scoring on taste, presentation, and creativity.

03

Give Back

Ticket sales, table sponsorships, and a silent auction raise funds that go directly to that year's beneficiary organization.

The Sweet 60

One shared ingredient. One shared purpose.

Every competing chef builds an original dish around honey — the thread that ties this year's competition together. It's a small, sweet detail with a big point: one ingredient, a dozen different visions, all in service of Central Florida seniors.

Guests taste, vote, and celebrate the creativity of chefs from senior living communities across the region — while every dollar raised goes directly to Seniors First.
