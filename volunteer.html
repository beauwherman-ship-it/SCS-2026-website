Event Day Shifts

Where we need help

2:00 – 5:00 PM
Setup

4:00 – 7:00 PM
Registration

Auction

General Volunteers

Welcome Team

7:00 – 8:00 PM
Cleanup
Help break down and pack up after the event wraps.
