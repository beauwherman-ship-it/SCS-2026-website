Where It Goes

What your support funds

Seniors First is the largest social services organization in metro Orlando, providing Meals on Wheels, in-home care, medical equipment loans, and community programs that help seniors live safely and independently. A gift of $180 funds one month of Meals on Wheels for a senior in need.

Meals on Wheels

Nutritious meals delivered directly to homebound seniors across Central Florida.

In-Home Care

Support services that help seniors age safely and independently in their own homes.

Community Programs

Medical equipment loans and additional programs addressing seniors' everyday needs.

Celebrating 60 Years

Founded in 1966, Seniors First marks its 60th anniversary in 2026. Senior Chef Showdown's Sweet 60 goal — $60,000 — represents a dollar for every year of service to our community.

Every donation, ticket, and sponsorship moves us closer to our Sweet 60 goal.
